How It Works

1. Pair Devices

Open KeyPad preferences and Mac Bluetooth settings. From your phone, tablet, or TV, connect to your Mac via Bluetooth.

2. Connect and Type

Select your device in KeyPad's menubar. Everything you type flows directly to it.

3. Instant Access

Hit ⌘ + Option + P anytime. KeyPad appears over any app.

4. Safe Disconnect

Switch away—KeyPad auto-disconnects, keeping your workflow uninterrupted.

FAQ

What's the hotkey?

⌘ + Option + P brings up KeyPad from anywhere.

Does it work with all devices?

Any Bluetooth device that accepts keyboard input: iPhone, iPad, Apple TV, smart TVs, laptops.

Is my data secure?

KeyPad has no internet access. It only uses Bluetooth for local input—no tracking, no ads.

Why initiate pairing from the device?

Standard Bluetooth flow ensures secure, reliable connections every time.
